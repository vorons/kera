## Kera 0.1.4: every model at its makers' own settings

**What this is.** Version 0.1.4, open source (Apache 2.0). Where you set nothing, a model now runs the way its makers
run it: music, more picture and video models, and chat models whose files carry no settings of their own. What you
set still wins, and nothing here trades quality for speed.

**Music (ACE-Step 1.5)**

- The base and SFT models (XL too) run at ACE-Step's own guidance 7 and shift 3. Before, they ran without guidance
  at shift 1. `kera music --guidance` and `--shift` set others.
- Turbo models get ACE-Step's own correction step (DCW), which ACE-Step turns on for them by default.
- With several models in the music folder, Kera uses the planner and the sound model ACE-Step's guide recommends
  for the computer's memory, at the highest precision that fits. It took the first by file name before: the base
  model, the smallest planner, the 4-bit file. `--lm` and `--dit` still choose.
- **Measured on a DGX Spark** (one folder with XL SFT, base and turbo models and two planners; three sets of lyrics,
  two seeds; the words Whisper hears): Kera 0.1.4 by itself, 95% of the lyrics; what 0.1.3 took by itself in the same
  folder, 75%; the same models as 0.1.4 at the old guidance and shift, 92%. The turbo correction step doesn't cost
  words (84% with it, 81% without, nine songs each).

**Pictures and video**

- 53 more models get their makers' settings by themselves, recognised by their makers' file names at any precision:
  Qwen-Image and its edit models, Z-Image, FLUX.2 klein, FLUX 3 klein, Stable Diffusion 3 and 3.5, SDXL base, Stable
  Diffusion 1.x and 2.x, Chroma, HunyuanVideo 1.5 and others. They ran at the image engine's generic 20 steps and
  guidance 7.
- Models whose makers use more steps now take them (Qwen-Image 50, SD 3.5 Medium 40, SDXL base 50, HunyuanVideo
  1.5 50), so they draw as their makers intend and **take longer**. `--steps` sets fewer.
- A speed-up variant, a fine-tune under another name or a community model keeps the engine's own settings.

**Chat**

- A model file that carries no sampling settings of its own gets its makers' own defaults (temperature, top_p,
  top_k, repetition penalty) from a list of 680 models built into Kera, collected from the makers' pages on Hugging
  Face. The model is found by its name and shape together, so a fine-tune under another name is left alone. Kera asks no
  server: the list is refreshed with each release. `--no-model-defaults` turns it off.

**Precision**

- A model's own weights are its full-precision file (BF16 or F16); 8-bit and 4-bit files are smaller copies. Kera
  says when the same model at full precision is next to the file you named and fits this computer's memory, and the
  help text says which files are which.

**Checked and unchanged.** Speech (Qwen3-TTS runs at its makers' temperature 0.9, top_k 50, top_p 1.0, which its
files carry), 3D (TRELLIS.2's own 12 steps) and sound effects (the model's own 100 steps, the full-precision file
first) already ran at their makers' settings.

**Not in this version.** Gated models (Llama, Gemma, Mistral) are in the chat list only where a public copy holds
the same settings file, byte for byte (Llama 3.2 1B and 3B and Llama 3 8B are; Gemma 3 and Llama 3.1 8B aren't); a
recent file of those models usually carries its settings itself. The lyric
numbers are from 6 to 9 short songs per setting on one machine, and single songs vary a lot: they show the direction,
not a precise size.
