## Kera 0.1.1: pictures, music, speech and video; faster engines; 45 bugs fixed

**What this is.** Version 0.1.1, open source (Apache 2.0). Besides chat, Kera now reads pictures, makes pictures,
songs, speech and short videos, all on your own computer. It starts as fast as stock llama.cpp from a cold start,
and fixes the 45 defects two full reviews found. The speed gates still wait for their measurements on the
Minisforum and the Mac (`docs/STATUS.md`). Everything not labelled lossy gives exactly the model's own answers.

**New**

- **Models that see images.** `kera serve -m model.gguf` finds a vision model's image reader (its "mmproj" file) by
  itself, next to the model or in Ollama's store, so apps can send it pictures through OpenAI's or Anthropic's API.
  The app lists your Ollama models by name and marks the ones that see images.
- **Models that make images.** A second engine, stable-diffusion.cpp, comes in the download's `image` folder.
  `kera image -m MODEL -p "a red apple on a wooden table" -o apple.png` makes a picture; `kera serve -m MODEL`
  serves an image model with OpenAI's images API (`/v1/images/generations`). SD 1.x/2.x/XL, SD3.5, FLUX, Qwen-Image,
  Z-Image and more, on Vulkan (Windows, Linux), Metal (Mac) or the CPU. Turbo models get their recommended settings
  (SD-Turbo: one step), which made a 512x512 picture 8 times faster than the engine's defaults, and right.
- **Music.** A third engine, acestep.cpp (ACE-Step 1.5), in the download's `music` folder.
  `kera music -p "upbeat pop rock with driving guitars" --duration 60 -o song.mp3` writes the lyrics (or uses
  yours, `--lyrics`) and makes a 48 kHz stereo song; `kera serve --music DIR` opens its own page for making songs.
  The models (3.3 GB at the smallest) come from huggingface.co/Serveurperso/ACE-Step-1.5-GGUF.
- **Speech.** `kera speak -m Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base-Q4_K_M.gguf -p "Hello" -o hello.wav`, with llama.cpp's own
  speech models (Qwen3-TTS in ten languages, a voice copied from a short sample; Pocket TTS). Its audio decoder is
  found by itself.
- **Video.** `kera video` makes a short .avi with Wan 2.1/2.2, LTX-Video and the other video models
  stable-diffusion.cpp runs.
- **Faster first load.** Kera used to read the whole model to fingerprint it while the model was loading; it now waits
  until the model is loaded. A cold start is as quick as stock llama.cpp's (about 10 s sooner the first time on a 23 GB
  model).

**Faster** (measured on a 4-core cloud CPU; `docs/BENCHMARKS.md`)

- Music is 1.8 times as fast: the music engine used only half the cores on CPUs whose cores run one thread each
  (Apple Silicon, many laptops); Kera now gives it all of them. A 10 s song: 40 s instead of 71 s.
- The image and music engines now pick the best code for your CPU when they start, as the chat engine does: the
  song's diffusion step is 1.7 times as fast with AVX-512, and they no longer need AVX2 to start at all.

**As accurate as the usual way** (`docs/QUALITY.md`)

- Answers: one model file through Kera and through Ollama 0.40.15, 200 questions with known answers (school maths
  and science): Kera 149 right, Ollama 147. The same prompt goes to the model either way.
- Pictures: SD-Turbo through Kera and through the model's own reference code, with the same seed, look identical
  with the same weights.

**Fixed** (`docs/NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md` N-014, among 45)

- A saved chat could be continued under different long-context (rope) settings, which could give wrong answers.
- A vision model could be given another model's image reader (a 3B model its 7B sibling's), which stopped it.
- Two quick clicks on Run crashed the app; on Windows, closing the app's window left the model running.
- The saved chats' disk budget now covers all models together (it could reach 20 GB per model).
- Windows users with an accented or non-Latin user name lost their machine profile and kept shaders, and couldn't
  make music or save speech.
- `kera music` into a folder that didn't exist lost the finished song and said it had worked.
- With `--backend auto`, a chat app that went away no longer leaves the engine working, and a failed swap no longer
  stops the server; expert streaming no longer stops every request after one failed read.

**Unchanged:** fine-tuning (`kera finetune`) is as in 0.1.0.
