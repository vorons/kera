## Kera 0.1.3: the model's own answers, the models' own settings

**What this is.** Version 0.1.3, open source (Apache 2.0). It adds no new kind of thing to make. It is about one
rule, the owner's rule number one: **Kera never changes how a model responds. It only makes things faster.** Every
place Kera touches a model's output was audited and checked against the stock engines; the places where Kera did
change something are fixed, and the modes that traded quality for speed are removed.

**The model's own answers**

- **No modes that trade quality for speed.** `kera serve --lossy-kv8`, `--lossy-route` and `--lossy-reuse` are
  removed. They were never on by default; now they don't exist.
- **At its defaults, Kera answers exactly as stock llama.cpp does**: every word and every probability, guessing
  ahead and saved chats included. This is now tested on every change (`docs/QUALITY.md`).
- **What you set wins.** `--temp`, `--top-p`, `--top-k` and `--min-p` (or llama.cpp's `LLAMA_ARG_*` variables) are no
  longer replaced by the model card's values. The card is used only for the card's own models, not for fine-tunes or
  look-alikes, and not when the file carries its own settings.
- **Saved chats** come back only into a run that would compute the same memory: not after an update, another
  context size on models that depend on it, other settings, or another adapter. A model file replaced by another is
  recognised.
- **Models from Ollama's store** run the way Ollama runs them, with their Modelfile's system prompt, settings and
  stop words. One without a chat format of its own is refused with a plain message instead of answering badly.
- **Images**: a model is no longer given an image reader that isn't its own. Word probabilities (`logprobs`) with
  guessing ahead are the model's own again.
- **Nothing added to your words, nothing cut**: `kera 3d -p` draws from your description as written; `kera music`
  lets the model choose the song's length; `kera sfx` passes a length only when you give one; no seed is fixed where
  the engine's own is random; the prompt improver asks the chat model as a chat app does and uses its whole answer.

**The models' own settings**

- **Pictures**: FLUX, FLUX.2, Z-Image-Turbo, SDXL-Lightning and SD-Turbo get their makers' steps and guidance, and
  the sampler and size their cards name, where you set none. FLUX is told apart by its weights, so a de-distilled
  model keeps its guidance.
- **Video**: Wan 2.1 and 2.2 and LTX 2.3 get their makers' steps, guidance, flow shift and Wan's default negative
  prompt. Wan ran at a generic guidance with no negative prompt before.
- These models now take their makers' step counts (FLUX.1-dev 50, Wan 2.1 50, Wan 2.2 40) instead of the engine's 20,
  so they draw as their makers intend and **take 2 to 2.5 times as long**. `--steps` sets fewer.
- **Chat**: Nemotron 3 Nano and Qwen3-Coder sample as their makers' engines do. `kera cli`, and a model given with
  `-hf`, get the card's settings as `kera serve -m` does.
- **Fine-tuning** defaults to about three passes over your data, puts examples in the model's chat format, and
  trains as hard on a Mac as elsewhere.

**Faster, with the same output**

- The picture, music, 3D and sound-effect engines keep the graphics chip's built programs in Kera's folder, so the
  first picture after a start isn't slow. `kera serve` with an image model warms up once after it starts, when the
  computer has a graphics chip. Not yet measured on one.
- A sound effect with a description used before skips loading its 2 GB text encoder. The sound is the same.

**Fixed**

- With `--backend auto`, a message waiting for the other back-end is answered after 90 seconds instead of failing
  after 10 minutes.
- Streamed experts: a failed read ends the request with an error instead of crashing the server.
- `kera music` writes WAV unless you ask for `.mp3`, and a lyrics file keeps its words in every text encoding.
- Windows users with an accented or non-Latin user name: the picture and sound engines' saved files now work.
- The rest are in `CHANGELOG.md`.

**Known, not fixed in this version** (planned for 0.1.4)

- **Music.** With ACE-Step's base and SFT models, `kera music` runs at the music engine's own values (50 steps, no
  guidance, shift 1), not the ones ACE-Step recommends, and takes the first planner model in the folder by name. A
  check found the sung words much less accurate that way. `--steps`, `--lm` and `--dit` can be set by hand; guidance
  and shift can't yet.
- **Models Kera doesn't know by name** run with their engine's defaults. Finding every model's own defaults
  automatically is being worked on.
- **The published speeds** (15-16 Oct, against Ollama and stock llama.cpp) are too high and will be measured again:
  each prompt was asked three times on one server, so Kera could copy its own earlier answer.

**Tested** on the CPU on every change: unit tests, the tiny-model exactness test, Kera against stock llama.cpp at
its defaults, saved chats, several chats at once, vision, pictures, the picture and video settings, music, speech,
3D and sound-effect commands, and fine-tuning. On a graphics chip (NVIDIA GB10, CUDA): Kera against stock llama.cpp,
saved chats, several chats at once, vision and pictures pass; in the exactness and guessing tests, two tiny random
test models pick another word at a near tie, where the chip rounds a batch of words differently from one at a time
(`docs/QUALITY.md`). **Not yet:** the speeds measured again on the Minisforum, the Mac and the Spark, and a real 3D
model on a graphics chip.
