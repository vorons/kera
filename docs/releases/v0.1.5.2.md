## Kera 0.1.5.2: LTX video follows what you ask; music loads 15 times sooner on a DGX Spark

**What this is.** Version 0.1.5.2 (0.1.5.1 with LTX guided the way its makers guide it), open source (Apache 2.0). Speed, where nothing changes what a model makes (every
file checked is the same as 0.1.4's, byte for byte), and LTX 2.5 video made to work. **The DGX Spark download comes first**, built and tested on
a DGX Spark; the Windows, Linux and Mac downloads stay at 0.1.4 until they are built.

**LTX video follows what you ask for** (found by the owner on a DGX Spark)

- A long clip no longer comes out as something else. A sea turtle over a reef was asked for and two people talking
  on a stage came out. The cause was the step schedule, which the image engine shifted by the clip's size where
  Lightricks' own pipelines give every clip the same one. The same clip, seed and settings now give the turtle.
- Lightricks' own guidance for LTX is in the image engine for those who want it (`kera video --help` gives the
  line). It is not on by itself: it takes four passes of the model a step where plain guidance takes two, and has
  not been checked on a long clip. The defaults are as in 0.1.5.1.
- `kera serve --sfx` and `kera music` refuse a port, a guidance or a shift that is not a number.

**LTX 2.5 video works** (0.1.5.1)

- It no longer stops at the first step: its small weights stored as bf16 are read as 32-bit floats, the same numbers
  exactly, so no converted copy of the model is needed.
- Its video is no longer blank: the text reader (Gemma 4) runs without fast attention on a graphics chip, where it
  gave values that are not numbers. The video model keeps its own fast attention.
- LTX videos are written at 24 frames a second (they were written at 16 and played slow), and LTX 2.5 takes 20
  steps where you set none.

**Music (ACE-Step 1.5)**

- The models load about 15 times sooner on a graphics chip that shares the computer's memory. On a DGX Spark, a
  60 s song with words from `kera music` takes **43 s instead of 76** with the XL SFT model and **24 s instead of
  about 50** with a turbo model; the first song from `kera serve --music` takes 42 s instead of 81.
- The songs are the same files, byte for byte. Only the loading changed: the weights went to the chip straight from
  the memory-mapped file at about 0.5 GB a second, and now go through ordinary memory, read by 8 threads.

**Sound effects**

- `kera serve --sfx DIR` keeps the sound-effect model loaded: on a DGX Spark a 10 s sound takes 32 s instead of 39,
  the same sound, sample for sample, as `kera sfx` makes.

**Measured, nothing changed**

- FLUX 3 klein on a DGX Spark, the makers' 16 steps at 1024x1024: a picture 23 s, an edit 50 s, a picture widened to
  1536x1024 67 s. Video (Wan 2.1, 33 frames, 50 steps): about 6 minutes. In both, the steps are 82 to 97% of the
  time and the engine's fast attention is already on; nothing was found that is faster with the same output.

**Known**

- The music change is timed on a DGX Spark only.
- GitHub's machines do not run for this repository at the moment, so this version was built and tested on the
  Spark itself and on a cloud machine, not by GitHub.
